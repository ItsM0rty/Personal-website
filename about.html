About

Hi, I'm Suyash.

I'm an AI engineer and full stack developer. I build LLM systems with guardrails and evals, and I do forward deployed work: sit with the people using the system, ship what they need, and leave something the next person can maintain.

Bio

What I do

I build LLM systems end to end: guardrails, evaluation pipelines, and the application code around them. I test against live providers and write evals that say what they cannot claim.

At Anterra Corp I fine-tuned YOLOv8m and integrated BoT-SORT tracking on Jetson Orin Nano edge hardware. I built a custom Nepali Devanagari ANPR model and demonstrated the traffic violation detection system to senior police and ministry officials.

On freelance projects I work directly with the people using the software. I have sat with factory workers to refine inventory workflows and with school administrators and teachers to fix bugs in an academic management system.
